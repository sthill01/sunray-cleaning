Discount FAQs

Questions about cleaning discounts.

Do weekly or biweekly cleaning clients get a discount?
Recurring clients may qualify for quote-based savings because Sun Ray can maintain the home more efficiently after the first cleaning visit and checklist setup.
Does Sun Ray Cleaning have referral discounts?
Referral thank-you credits may be available when a referred customer books a qualifying clean. Ask about the current referral offer when you call or text.
Are there discounts for Airbnb hosts with multiple properties?
Yes, Sun Ray can review multi-property pricing, repeat turnover support and priority scheduling for hosts and property managers in Park City, Heber City, Midway and nearby areas.
Can I combine a special with a recurring cleaning discount?
Sometimes, but not always. Sun Ray confirms eligibility, timing and whether offers can be combined before the cleaning is scheduled.
