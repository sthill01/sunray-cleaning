# Review Request Templates (Sun Ray Cleaning Services)

Goal: increase review volume and “service + city” specificity (Park City / Heber City / Midway / Summit County / Wasatch County) without pressuring customers or asking for specific star ratings.

Guidelines:

- Ask for an honest review (do not ask for “5-star”).
- Do not offer incentives for reviews.
- Make it easy: one link + one short prompt.

Replace the placeholders below:

- `{FIRST_NAME}`
- `{CITY_OR_NEIGHBORHOOD}`
- `{SERVICE}` (deep clean / recurring / move-in/out / STR turnover)
- `{GBP_REVIEW_LINK}` (direct “leave a review” link)

---

## SMS (same day, after the clean)

Hi {FIRST_NAME} — thank you for having Sun Ray Cleaning Services out today. If you have 30 seconds, would you leave an honest Google review? It really helps local homeowners and hosts find us.

If you can, please mention the service and area (example: “{SERVICE} in {CITY_OR_NEIGHBORHOOD}”).
{GBP_REVIEW_LINK}

## SMS (48 hours later, if no response)

Hi {FIRST_NAME} — quick follow-up from Sun Ray Cleaning Services. If everything still looks great, would you be willing to share a short Google review? Even 1–2 sentences helps.

Mentioning the service + area is perfect: “{SERVICE} in {CITY_OR_NEIGHBORHOOD}.”
{GBP_REVIEW_LINK}

---

“If you’re happy with how everything turned out, an honest Google review helps us a lot — especially if you mention the service and the area, like Park City or Heber City.”
