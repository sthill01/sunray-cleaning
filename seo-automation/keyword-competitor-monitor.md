## Ranking Dimensions To Track

- Sun Ray appears or does not appear.
- Competitors mentioned above Sun Ray.
- Pages cited, directories cited, or review platforms cited.
- Keyword intent: commercial, informational, local, pricing, seasonal, comparison.
- Opportunity score:
  - Low-hanging fruit: low competitor specificity, neighborhood/service modifier, weak current content.
  - Conversion impact: pricing, booking, emergency/move/STR, luxury/second-home intent.
