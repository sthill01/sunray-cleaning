# Sun Ray Local SEO + LLM Visibility Check

Run date: 2026-05-04 (America/Denver)

Scope: residential home cleaning, Airbnb/VRBO cleaning, deep cleaning, recurring cleaning, move-in/move-out in Park City, Midway, Heber City, Summit County (UT), Wasatch County (UT).

Priority query set source: `seo-automation/keyword-competitor-monitor.md`

Important note on rankings: This snapshot is based on non-personalized web index results and directory visibility proxies. Exact Google local-pack ranking (Maps 3-pack) is **not** verified here due to personalization, location, and tool constraints.

## 1) Summary of wins/losses vs prior check (2026-04-27)

### Wins / improvements (proxy)

- Sun Ray owned-property visibility remains solid: the homepage + Park City + Midway/Heber pages are indexed and show up for broad “cleaning services Park City / Midway / Heber” discovery queries. (Proxy evidence: the Park City and Midway/Heber location pages are surfaced in search and include service-modifier blocks + FAQ content.)  
- The Midway/Heber location page copy is aligned to “cabins / vacation properties” language (good for Midway intent), even though “cabin cleaning Midway” SERPs still skew to third-party marketplaces.

### Losses / unchanged constraints

- Sun Ray is still not consistently visible on STR-intent, non-brand queries like “airbnb cleaning park city”, “vrbo cleaning park city utah”, and “vacation rental cleaning park city” where competitors have dedicated STR landing pages and/or strong directory signals.
- Entity ambiguity remains a risk: “Sun Ray Cleaning” is shared by unrelated businesses (example: an Arizona “Sun Ray Cleaning” site surfaced in some searches). This increases the value of GBP + consistent citations + `sameAs` schema.

### Notable competitor landscape change (practical impact)

- More “Park City + cleaning” competitors and city landing pages are showing up in proxies (examples: Real Clean, Sky House Cleaning, BR Services, AAA Housekeeping), which increases the bar for non-brand category visibility.

## 2) Visibility proxy notes by priority query set

This is a **directional** read on who shows up most often and what types of pages get returned (service pages vs directory profiles).

### Park City STR intent

Keywords: `airbnb cleaning park city`, `vacation rental cleaning park city`, `short term rental cleaning park city`, `vrbo cleaning park city utah`

Commonly surfaced pages/sources:
- Clean Casa: dedicated Park City Airbnb cleaning landing page.
- High Mountain Luxury Cleaning: dedicated “Park City vacation rental cleaning” emphasis on homepage and service blocks.
- American Housekeeping of Utah: Park City vacation rental + Airbnb turnover page.
- Park City-specific STR operators (e.g., Sten Family Cleaning) and marketplaces (e.g., Turno).

Sun Ray:
- The Sun Ray Park City page includes STR language (“Short-Term Rentals / AirBnB”) and FAQ content, but a dedicated STR landing page targeted to Park City still looks like the fastest way to compete for these phrases (plus a pricing anchor).

### Park City general + service modifiers

Keywords: `house cleaning park city ut`, `deep cleaning park city`, `recurring cleaning park city`, `move out cleaning park city`

Commonly surfaced pages/sources:
- Park City House Cleaning (brand-match domain).
- Wasatch Cleaning’s Park City service page (covers deep/move-in/out/routine).
- Large-area competitors + city landing pages (examples: Queen of Maids, AAA Housekeeping, BR Services).
- Directories/marketplaces (Care.com cost page, Homeaglow).

Sun Ray:
- Sun Ray appears in proxies for broader “cleaning services” queries and sometimes for the above, but not as consistently as the Park City-branded operators.

### Heber City / Midway

Keywords: `house cleaning heber city ut`, `move out cleaning heber`, `house cleaning midway utah`, `cabin cleaning midway utah`

Commonly surfaced pages/sources:
- Heber City Cleaning (service pages for house cleaning + move-in/out).
- Chikas Cleaners (Heber City location page and “AirBnB cleaning” positioning).
- Wasatch-wide competitors with Heber/Midway landing pages.
- Marketplaces: Homeaglow; Turno (for Midway/STR cleaners).

Sun Ray:
- The combined Midway/Heber page is discoverable and uses “cabins / vacation properties” language, but “Heber City” still benefits from a distinct Heber-only page (or at least a Heber-first page title/H1) to avoid query dilution.

### County-level

Keywords: `summit county house cleaning`, `wasatch county house cleaning`

Commonly surfaced pages/sources:
- High Mountain Luxury Cleaning’s “Summit County / Wasatch County house cleaning” landing pages.
- Regional operators with explicit county hubs (plus service-area lists).

Sun Ray:
- Sun Ray does not currently have a dedicated Summit County hub page or Wasatch County hub page in the visible proxy set; these are good “easy wins” because they help internal linking and AI citations (clear geography model).
