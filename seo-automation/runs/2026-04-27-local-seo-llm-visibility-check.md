## Executive Snapshot

### Sun Ray (sunray-cleaning.com)

- Strong brand / owned-property visibility: the official site pages are discoverable for “cleaning services Park City” style queries and brand lookups.
- Weak-to-moderate “category + city” visibility for the highest-conversion, non-brand queries (Airbnb/VRBO turnover, move-out cleaning, deep cleaning, recurring cleaning) compared to established competitors and aggregators.
- Current third-party visibility is present (example: Thumbtack profile), but broader citation coverage appears thin or inconsistent (and the “Sun Ray Cleaning” name is shared by unrelated businesses in other states, which can confuse AI answers if entity signals are weak).

### Competitors (core list)

- Park City House Cleaning: strong “Park City” brand-match domain and content footprint.
- Clean Casa: heavy content production around STR cleaning in Park City, including “project” examples with prices and fee data pages.
- High Mountain Luxury Cleaning (High Mountain Cleaning Services): strong Summit/Wasatch positioning + “luxury” angle.
- Heber City Cleaning: clear “city + service” positioning with “starting at” pricing callouts and dedicated service pages.
- Chikas Cleaners: strong service-line clarity (AirBnB / move in-out) and clear Heber City base.
- Wasatch Cleaning: multiple similarly named entities; some have broad “Wasatch Front” coverage including Park City.
- American Housekeeping of Utah: long-established “since 1991” positioning with “Park City to Heber” contact routing.

## What Competitors Are Doing That LLMs Tend to Reward

Patterns seen on competitors that frequently get cited or rank well:

- Publishing STR-specific content: “turnover”, “same-day”, “checklists”, “what’s included”, and “pricing examples”.
- Clear city + service alignment in headings and page slugs (e.g., “Airbnb cleaning in Park City”).
- Visible pricing anchors (“starting at $120”, project totals, fee data pages).
- Strong trust signals: years-in-business, bonded/insured/licensed language, and prominent reviews/testimonials.
