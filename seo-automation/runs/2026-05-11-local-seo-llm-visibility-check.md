## 1) Summary of wins/losses vs prior check (2026-05-04)

### Wins / improvements (proxy)

- Owned-property visibility remains solid: Sun Ray’s homepage and location pages are indexed and discoverable for brand + city queries (example: Park City page surfaces reliably when searching for Sun Ray + Park City).
- The Park City page includes strong service-line coverage (deep clean, recurring, move-in/out, STR turnover) plus on-page FAQs/checklist content, which is helpful for AI-snippet eligibility.

### Losses / unchanged constraints

- Sun Ray is still not commonly surfaced on the highest-conversion non-brand queries (especially STR intent like “airbnb cleaning park city” / “vacation rental cleaning park city”) where competitors have city+service matched landing pages and stronger third-party citation footprints.
- Entity ambiguity persists: search results still surface unrelated “Sun Ray Cleaning” entities (notably Arizona), which raises the importance of GBP + citation consistency + `sameAs` schema.
- Directory signals still appear to lean “Herriman / Salt Lake County” in some third-party profiles, which conflicts with the Wasatch Back positioning (Park City / Heber / Midway / Summit & Wasatch counties).

### Notable competitor landscape movement (practical impact)

- Additional Park City/Heber cleaning operators are visible in proxies beyond the core list (examples: Venegas Home Cleaning, Home Glow, Assured Properties, Real Clean, Stunning Cleaning Services). This increases the content + citation bar for category keywords.
