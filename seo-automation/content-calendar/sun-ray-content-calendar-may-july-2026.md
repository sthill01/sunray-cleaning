- Use GBP Updates for regular service proof, seasonal reminders, and local-area posts.

- Attach a real photo whenever possible, preferably a cleaned room from the same area or service theme.
- Use JPG or PNG photos, clean crops, no heavy text overlays, and no private client identifiers.
