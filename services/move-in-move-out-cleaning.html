Move cleaning FAQs

Common move-in and move-out questions.

What is included in a move-out clean?
Move-out cleaning includes kitchens, bathrooms, floors, closets, cabinet surfaces, baseboards, high-touch areas and appliance interiors when requested.
How far in advance should I book?
Booking 1 to 2 weeks ahead is best, especially May through September. Shorter notice may be available depending on the schedule.
Can you clean on moving day?
Yes, when scheduling allows. Move-out cleaning is most effective after furniture and belongings have been removed.
