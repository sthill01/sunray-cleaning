Deep cleaning FAQs

Common deep cleaning questions.

What is the difference between regular cleaning and deep cleaning?
Regular cleaning maintains a home. Deep cleaning is more detailed and targets buildup, fixtures, appliance interiors, baseboards and areas that do not get attention every visit.
How often should I schedule a deep clean?
Most homes benefit from deep cleaning every 3 to 6 months, seasonally for vacation homes, before hosting or before starting recurring service.
Do you clean inside ovens and refrigerators?
Yes. Interior appliance cleaning can be included in a deep clean. Mention appliances when requesting your quote.
