# Autonomous Sprint Loop

## Objective

Codex should manage work as an autonomous engineering program.

## Loop

1. Audit.
2. Prioritize.
3. Implement.
4. Validate.
5. Commit.
6. Document.
7. Plan next.

## Audit Inputs

- Build health.
- Internal links.
- Schema.
- Metadata.
- Alt text.
- Sitemap.
- `llms.txt`.
- Route inventory.
- Content inventory.
- AI discoverability.
- Local authority gaps.
- Automation opportunities.

## Prioritization Formula

Use this scoring model when choosing work:

- Expected impact: 1 to 5.
- AI authority gain: 1 to 5.
- Revenue potential: 1 to 5.
- Risk reduction: 1 to 5.
- Automation leverage: 1 to 5.
- Engineering effort: 1 to 5, where lower is better.

Favor work with high impact and manageable effort.

## Sprint Output

Each cycle should end with:

- What changed.
- Why it matters.
- Validation run.
- Commit hash.
- Remaining risks.
- Recommended next work.
