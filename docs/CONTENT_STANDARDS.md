# Content Standards

Date: 2026-06-30

## Editorial Standard

Content should be useful, specific, locally grounded, and backed by structured
evidence in the repository.

Sun Ray should sound like a local, trustworthy cleaning company with strong
operational standards. Avoid generic filler, exaggerated claims, and unsupported
guarantees.

## Page Types

### Service Pages

Service pages should explain:

- Who the service is for.
- What is included.
- What affects the quote.
- Which local areas are served.
- Related services.
- Relevant FAQs.
- Relevant photos.
- Quote path.

### Location Pages

Location pages should explain:

- The local service area.
- Relevant home types.
- Service needs in that location.
- Related neighborhoods.
- Relevant services.
- Relevant FAQs.
- Relevant photos.
- Quote path.

### Blog And Resource Pages

Articles should answer real customer questions and support service or location
authority. They should link to the relevant service page, location page, and
quote path.

### FAQ Content

FAQs should answer specific questions customers or answer engines are likely to
ask. Avoid duplicating the same generic FAQ across every page unless build-time
generation has a deliberate reason.

### Review Content

Reviews should be used as trust proof. Quote only approved public reviews and
preserve their source.

## Content Quality Rules

- Do not create doorway pages.
- Do not create thin location swaps.
- Do not claim services, locations, hours, certifications, guarantees, or
  pricing that are not documented.
- Prefer reusable content systems when repeating patterns.
- Keep internal links natural and helpful.
