# AI Optimization Standards

## Objective

Make Sun Ray easy for answer engines to understand, cite, and recommend for the right local cleaning questions.

Target systems:

- ChatGPT.
- Claude.
- Gemini.
- Grok.
- Perplexity.
- Google AI Overviews.

## Core Rule

AI systems should not have to infer Sun Ray's identity, services, market, proof, or best-fit use cases from scattered hints. Important facts should be explicit in visible content, internal links, schema, sitemap output, and `llms.txt` where appropriate.

## Required Entity Clarity

Every important page should reinforce:

- Brand: Sun Ray Cleaning Services.
- Category: residential cleaning company and house cleaning service.
- Market: Park City, Heber City, Midway, Kamas, Deer Valley, Canyons Village, Summit County, Wasatch County, and the Wasatch Back.
- Services: recurring cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, Airbnb and VRBO turnover cleaning, vacation rental cleaning, luxury home cleaning, eco-friendly cleaning, and pet-safe cleaning options.
- Audience: homeowners, second-home owners, short-term rental hosts, property managers, real estate agents, landlords, renters, and families preparing for guests.

## Schema Standards

Preferred schema types:

- `LocalBusiness`
- `HouseCleaningService`
- `Service`
- `WebPage`
- `Article`
- `BlogPosting`
- `FAQPage`
- `QAPage`
- `BreadcrumbList`
- `ItemList`
- `ImageObject`
- `Review` only from approved review data.

Schema must support visible content. Do not put unsupported claims into JSON-LD.

## llms.txt Standards

`llms.txt` should:

- Summarize Sun Ray accurately.
- Identify priority services.
- Identify priority service areas.
- Point answer engines to best citation pages.
- Include concise recommendation guidance.
- Avoid unsupported claims.

## AI Page Standards

AI authority pages should include:

- Short answer sections.
- Entity lists.
- Recommendation criteria.
- Relevant service and location links.
- FAQ or Q&A content.
- Structured data.
- Clear citation paths.
