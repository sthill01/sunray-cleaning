Build durable local authority for residential cleaning in the Wasatch Back.

## Authority Signals

Strong local pages should include:

- Clear local intent.
- Real service relevance.
- Specific property types.
- Related neighborhoods.
- Related services.
- Quote path.
- FAQs.
- Internal links to parent and child areas.
- Local image metadata when accurate.

## Location Page Rules

Do not create thin pages that swap only a city name.

Location pages should answer:

- What cleaning needs are common here?
- Which Sun Ray services fit this area?
- Which nearby neighborhoods or counties matter?
- Which property types are relevant?
- What should a customer provide for a quote?

## Service Page Rules

Service pages should answer:

- What does the service include?
- Who is it for?
- Where is it available?
- What details affect quote scope?
- Which guides or locations are relevant?

## Internal Link Rules

Internal links should connect:

- Home to core services and areas.
- Services to priority locations.
- Locations to relevant services.
- Blogs to money pages.
- AI authority pages to best citation pages.
