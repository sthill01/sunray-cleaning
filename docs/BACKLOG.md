## Scoring Model

Score each item from 1 to 5:

- Impact.
- AI authority gain.
- Revenue potential.
- Risk reduction.
- Automation leverage.
- Effort, where lower is better.

Prioritize work with high authority, trust, revenue, risk, or automation value and manageable effort.
