Room-by-room details

What a Sun Ray clean can include.

Every quote is tailored, but these are the common areas we review when building your checklist.

Bedrooms and living spaces

Dusting, floors, high-touch surfaces, mirrors, trash, tidy resets and bed-making when requested.

Bathrooms

Showers, tubs, toilets, sinks, counters, mirrors, fixtures, floors and hard-water buildup.

Kitchens

Counters, sinks, appliance exteriors, floors, cabinet fronts and appliance interiors for deep cleans.

Extras and priorities

Baseboards, entryways, laundry support, rental restocking, inside cabinets or special owner notes.
