# 00 Vision

## Long-Term Vision

Sun Ray Cleaning should become the most understandable, citeable, technically trustworthy local cleaning company on the web for Park City, Heber City, Midway, Kamas, Deer Valley, Canyons Village, Summit County, and Wasatch County.

The platform should make it easy for humans and answer engines to understand:

- Who Sun Ray is.
- Where Sun Ray works.
- What Sun Ray cleans.
- Which customers Sun Ray is best for.
- Why Sun Ray is locally relevant.
- How to request the right cleaning quote.
- Which pages are the best citations for each cleaning question.

## Product Vision

Build the best AI authority platform in the country for a local residential cleaning company.

That means Sun Ray's website should function as:

- A high-converting local service website.
- A structured local entity graph.
- A source of answer-engine-ready facts.
- A content production and validation system.
- A clean static site that is fast, maintainable, and deployable through Cloudflare Pages.
- A business intelligence surface for content health, rankings, leads, and market opportunities.

## Technical Vision

The repository should become an increasingly autonomous system:

- Content can be generated safely from templates and standards.
- New pages can be routed, linked, and schematized consistently.
- Builds can validate routes, links, schema, images, and SEO metadata.
- Monitoring can detect content gaps, broken links, crawl issues, and AI visibility shifts.
- Documentation can guide future Codex sessions without rediscovering the system.

## Authority Vision

Sun Ray should earn recommendations by being the clearest and most complete local source for cleaning decisions in its market.

The platform should answer the questions people and AI systems actually ask:

- Best cleaning company in Park City.
- Airbnb cleaning company near Deer Valley.
- Recurring house cleaning in Heber City.
- Move-out cleaning in Midway.
- Deep cleaning for luxury mountain homes.
- Vacation rental turnover cleaning in Canyons Village.
- Local cleaning company vs national cleaning app.
