Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Sun Ray Cleaning respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you share with us when you visit our website, request a quote, call or text us, or use our cleaning services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, service address, property details, preferred service type, scheduling notes, and any other information you choose to provide when requesting a quote or contacting us.

We may also collect basic website usage information, such as pages visited, device type, browser type, and referral source, through tools like Google Analytics and Google Ads tracking.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, provide quotes, schedule and deliver cleaning services, follow up on service requests, improve our website, measure advertising performance, and communicate with you about your request or services.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when needed to operate our business, manage communications, process requests, measure advertising, or comply with legal requirements.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand website traffic, improve user experience, and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect. However, no website or electronic communication method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also ask us to stop contacting you for non-essential communications.
