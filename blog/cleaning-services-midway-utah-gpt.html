What Makes Midway Homes Unique (and Why That Matters for Cleaning)

Midway isn't Park City. The vibe is quieter, more neighborhood-y, more rooted. Many homes here are primary residences with families and dogs, not just investment properties. But there are also hundreds of vacation rentals — Swiss-inspired chalets, newer luxury builds near Soldier Hollow, cozy cabins along the Provo River corridor — and they all need consistent, reliable cleaning between guests.

A few things make Midway properties stand out from a cleaning perspective:

Hard water is relentless. Heber Valley water is mineral-rich, which means calcium and limescale buildup shows up fast — on faucets, inside dishwashers, around tub jets in the hot-pot-inspired soaking tubs that many local vacation rentals feature. Standard cleaning products don't cut it. We use professional-grade descaling treatments that actually work without damaging fixtures.

Seasonal transitions are dramatic. Midway winters are cold and snowy; summers are warm and dusty. Each transition brings its own mess — mud season in spring, cottonwood fluff in early summer, wildfire smoke particulates later in the season. Homes here benefit from a seasonal deep clean that addresses what each time of year brings in, not just a once-a-year scrub.

Outdoor living means more interior mess. Midway's outdoor culture — golf, fishing, hiking, hot pot soaking, snowshoeing — means guests and families track in more than most. Mud rooms, entryways, and hardwood or tile floors take a beating. We pay special attention to these transition areas because they set the tone for how clean the rest of the home feels.

Vacation Rental Cleaning in Midway: What Hosts Actually Need

If you're a vacation rental host in Midway, you already know the pressure. A guest checks out at 10 a.m., the next one arrives at 3 p.m., and in between, the entire property needs to look impeccable. It's not just about being clean — it's about looking clean, smelling clean, and having everything reset exactly right so the next guest starts their stay with a great impression.

Sun Ray offers dedicated Airbnb and VRBO turnover cleaning throughout Midway and Heber Valley. Here's what that typically includes:

  • Full bathroom sanitation — toilets, showers, tubs, sinks, mirrors, floors
  • Kitchen reset — dishes checked, counters wiped, appliances cleaned inside and out, trash removed
  • Linen management — stripping and replacing beds (we can coordinate with your laundry service or handle it directly)
  • Vacuuming and mopping all floors
  • Trash removal from all rooms
  • Restocking supplies (paper products, toiletries) if you leave them staged
  • A final walkthrough to check for anything left behind or out of place

We also communicate. If we notice something broken, a missing amenity, or a maintenance issue, we'll flag it to you directly — not leave you to discover it when the next guest complains. That kind of proactive communication is something big cleaning franchises rarely offer, but it's standard for us.

Deep Cleaning for Midway Vacation Homes and Seasonal Properties

Many Midway properties are used seasonally — families who come for ski season, then again in summer, or vacation homes that sit empty for months at a stretch. After any extended vacancy, a deep clean is essential before your family arrives or before you open back up for guests.

A deep clean from Sun Ray is more thorough than a standard recurring clean. We get inside cabinets, behind appliances, under furniture, and into the grout lines. We dust ceiling fans and light fixtures, clean baseboards, wipe down every door and cabinet face, and give bathrooms and kitchens a full reset. If the property has been closed up through a Utah winter, there's often moisture, dust, and hard water residue that needs addressing before anything else.

We recommend scheduling your seasonal deep clean at least two weeks before your first planned occupancy — either by your own family or by rental guests. Summer weekends book up fast in Heber Valley, and we want to make sure you have time to address anything that comes up after the clean.
