Sundance Film Festival brings tens of thousands of visitors to Park City every January, creating some of the most intense turnover demand of the year. Here is how hosts prepare their properties.

Why Sundance is different from regular ski season

Sundance runs for roughly 10 days in late January, overlapping with peak ski season. Park City's rental inventory gets fully absorbed—every house, condo, and apartment in Old Town, Deer Valley, Park Meadows, and nearby neighborhoods fills with festival guests, production crews, and industry attendees.

Festival guests differ from ski guests. They keep late hours, entertain in the home, and may have larger group sizes due to budget sharing. The result is heavier-than-normal wear on kitchens, living areas, and bathrooms over a short window, followed by a rapid checkout as the next group arrives within hours.

Turnovers during Sundance: what to plan for

Most Sundance hosts do one large move-out/move-in clean rather than multiple day-of turnovers—guests typically book the full festival run. That means one deep turnaround on the Saturday or Sunday after festival close, followed by a quick same-day reset if a ski-only guest follows immediately.

Expect kitchen deep-clean scope: inside oven, inside refrigerator if guests had extended stays, full cabinet and counter reset. Expect heavier-than-usual bathroom cleaning. Any wine or food spills on carpets or furniture should be noted and addressed before the next guests arrive.

Pre-Sundance prep cleaning

Many experienced Park City hosts schedule a pre-Sundance deep clean in early January to start the festival period with the home in pristine condition. This reduces stress during the tight festival-close turnaround and ensures any issues (hard water buildup, appliance residue, winter grit) are addressed before high-expectation festival guests arrive.

Booking: why December is the deadline

Park City cleaning availability in January is limited. Most established local cleaners—including Sun Ray—fill their January calendars before December ends. Hosts who wait until January to find cleaning help for Sundance often cannot get reliable service on short notice. If Sundance is a regular booking on your property, lock in your cleaner each year before the holidays.

FAQs about Sundance Film Festival rental cleaning

When should I book cleaning for Sundance?
Book by December 1 at the latest. Sun Ray recommends contacting in October or November to confirm dates, especially if you need both a pre-Sundance deep clean and a post-festival turnaround.
What is the typical scope for a post-Sundance turnaround?
A full deep-clean scope: inside appliances, kitchen reset, bathroom scrub, linen change on all beds, carpet and floor cleaning, trash removal, and a photo walkthrough. Budget for more time than a standard turnover—festival stays are harder on homes.
Do you serve all Park City neighborhoods during Sundance?
Yes. Sun Ray covers Old Town, Deer Valley, Park Meadows, Canyons Village, Prospector, Silver Creek, and nearby areas during Sundance and year-round.
