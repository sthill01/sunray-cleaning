What Does a Deep Clean Include? A Room-by-Room Breakdown

A deep clean targets buildup and detail work beyond routine maintenance. The exact checklist depends on your home's condition, surfaces, rooms, and priorities.

Sun Ray CleaningPark City / Heber / MidwayUpdated August 2026
Get a quoteBack to blog

Start with the rooms and buildup that matter most. Sun Ray confirms the final checklist during the quote process, including any add-on areas or surfaces that need special care.

What Separates a Deep Clean from Regular Cleaning?

A recurring cleaning service maintains frequently used surfaces from week to week. A deep clean adds detail work such as baseboards, edges, fixtures, and buildup in less frequently cleaned areas.

In Summit County and Wasatch County, common priorities include dry-air dust, snow-season grit, hard-water residue, fireplaces, pets, and homes that sit empty between visits.

The Kitchen: Where Deep Cleaning Makes the Biggest Difference

Kitchen deep cleaning typically focuses on grease, crumbs, spills, cabinet fronts, backsplash areas, appliance exteriors, counters, sinks, fixtures, and floors.

Oven interiors, refrigerator interiors, inside cabinets, and other appliance details can be added when requested. Confirm these tasks in advance so the quote includes the needed time and access.

For Park City homes with hard-water residue, the team reviews the affected fixture or surface before choosing a cleaning method. Some long-standing mineral damage may need specialty restoration rather than routine cleaning.

Bathrooms: Tackling Hard Water, Mold, and Hidden Grime

Bathrooms often need extra attention around showers, tubs, toilets, sinks, mirrors, fixtures, grout, floors, and hard-water residue. These are common priorities in Heber City and Midway homes.

A bathroom checklist may add baseboards, vent covers, mirror edges, shower-door detail, fixture buildup, and the areas behind or beside the toilet and vanity. Delicate stone, glass, or specialty finishes are reviewed before treatment.

Inside drawers and cabinets, jetted-tub plumbing, or other specialty tasks are not assumed. Add them to the quote request if they are priorities.

Bedrooms and Closets: More Than Making the Bed

Bedroom deep cleaning can add baseboards, door frames, fan blades, switch plates, window sills, tracks, closet floors, and reachable areas beneath light furniture.

Tell the team about delicate items, heavy furniture, locked closets, or rooms that should be skipped. Bed-making, linen changes, closet shelves, and mattress vacuuming should be confirmed when the quote is prepared.

In Park City vacation homes that sit empty between stays, bedroom priorities often include settled dust, guest-ready presentation, and a careful check of floors, surfaces, and linens.

Living Areas and Common Spaces

Living-area deep cleaning can include detailed dusting, baseboards, doors, frames, railings, reachable shelving, floor edges, and vacuuming beneath removable sofa cushions.

Fireplace mantels and exterior hearth surfaces can be included after the material and condition are reviewed. Ash removal, chimney work, stain restoration, and specialty stone treatment are separate from routine surface cleaning.

Interior window glass, tracks, blinds, and detailed railing work vary by scope. Include the number and type of windows or specialty features when requesting a quote.

If you are starting a recurring cleaning schedule, an initial deep clean can address existing buildup before maintenance visits begin.

Utility Spaces, Laundry Rooms, and Entryways

Laundry rooms may include appliance exteriors, reachable shelving, utility sinks, floors, baseboards, and surface dust. Moving large appliances is not assumed and depends on safe access.

Mudrooms and entries often need floor detail, salt or de-icer residue removal, cubbies, benches, hooks, boot trays, and pet-area cleanup.

Garages are quoted separately when requested. For Heber City homes, describe whether the need is sweeping, cobweb removal, reachable surface wiping, or a larger cleanout.

When Should You Schedule a Deep Clean?

Useful times for a deep clean include seasonal openings, holiday preparation, owner arrivals, the start of recurring service, or a move-in or move-out. Rental owners in Park City and Midway may also schedule deeper work before busy guest seasons.

There is no single schedule for every home. Usage, pets, surfaces, guest traffic, vacancy, and the level of routine maintenance all affect how often deeper work is useful.

If you are unsure which service fits, request a quote with the square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, pets, current condition, last deep-clean date, timing, and priority areas. Sun Ray serves homes throughout Summit County and Wasatch County.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a deep clean take compared to a regular cleaning?

Timing depends on square footage, rooms, condition, access, surfaces, and the requested checklist. Share those details for a more accurate time and price estimate.

Do I need to be home during the deep clean?

Not always. Many Park City and Heber City clients provide approved door-code or lockbox access. Be reachable by phone for questions about surfaces, delicate items, pets, or areas to skip.

Will a deep clean remove hard water stains from my glass shower doors?

Some mineral residue can be improved with cleaning, but results depend on the surface and how long the buildup has been present. Etched or damaged glass may need specialty restoration.

What's the difference between a deep clean and a move-out clean?

A move-in or move-out clean is scoped for an empty or transitioning property. It may add inside cabinets, closets, appliances, and other handoff priorities, but the final checklist should be confirmed before the visit.

How often should I schedule a deep clean if I also have recurring cleaning?

It depends on the home. Usage, pets, guests, vacancy, surfaces, and the recurring checklist all affect whether deeper work is useful seasonally or only for specific priorities.
