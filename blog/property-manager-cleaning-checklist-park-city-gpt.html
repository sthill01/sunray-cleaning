Property managers overseeing Park City short-term rentals need consistent, reliable cleaning across multiple properties and seasons. Here is a practical checklist and framework for STR cleaning management.

Turnover cleaning checklist by room

Entry and mudroom: Sweep and mop, wipe boot trays, check coat hooks and storage, remove trash. Kitchen: Wipe all counters and appliances, clean stovetop, check inside microwave, run or empty dishwasher, restock dish soap and hand soap, check and clean refrigerator exterior. Bathrooms (each): Scrub toilet inside and out, scrub tub or shower, wipe sink and counter, polish fixtures, replace towels, check and restock toiletries per property supply kit. Bedrooms (each): Full linen change, pillow covers and mattress cover inspected, surfaces dusted, floor vacuumed, closets checked for left items, nightstand and lamp wiped. Living area: Surfaces dusted, floors vacuumed or mopped, remote controls located and set out, fireplace glass wiped if applicable. Final walkthrough: Check all windows locked, HVAC set correctly, doors secured, exterior entry clear.

Deep clean schedule for managed properties

STR properties under active management should receive a full deep clean at minimum twice per year: once at ski season close (April) and once before ski season opens (November). High-occupancy properties benefit from a third deep clean in late summer before fall transitions. Deep cleans address inside appliances, grout, baseboards, window tracks, descaling, and cabinet interiors that routine turnovers do not cover.

How to vet a cleaning vendor for your managed portfolio

Check that the cleaner is insured and can provide a certificate of insurance. Ask for references from other property managers, not just residential clients. Confirm the cleaner is familiar with the specific neighborhood and property access logistics (gate codes, keyboxes, building entry). Discuss how they handle discovered damage, left items, and supply restocking. Clarify the cancellation and no-show policy before your first busy weekend.

Communication and documentation standards

Good property managers require cleaning confirmation after each turnover, a photo check-out summary, and immediate notification of any damage or issues discovered. Set these expectations with your cleaning vendor before the first season. Sun Ray can provide text confirmation, photo summaries, and same-day issue flagging as part of a property management cleaning arrangement.

FAQs for property managers using Sun Ray Cleaning

Does Sun Ray work with property managers handling multiple properties?
Yes. Sun Ray works with property managers across Park City, Heber City, and Midway. Multi-property arrangements are discussed during the onboarding call to align on scheduling, access, standards, and communication preferences.
Can Sun Ray handle same-day turnovers during peak season?
Yes, with advance scheduling. Peak ski season and Sundance require bookings well in advance—ideally 4–8 weeks or on a recurring contract basis. Last-minute same-day turnovers during February powder weeks are difficult to accommodate without a standing arrangement.
How does Sun Ray handle discovered damage or guest-left items?
Sun Ray will photograph and immediately notify the property manager of any damage, left items, or supply shortages discovered during a turnover. Guest-left items are set aside and not discarded without owner authorization.
