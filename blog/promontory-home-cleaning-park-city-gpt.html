Promontory is one of Utah’s premier gated communities, and its homes reflect that. Here is what cleaning looks like for owners who expect detail, discretion, and consistency.

What sets Promontory properties apart

Promontory sits northeast of Park City proper, occupying a high-elevation plateau with long mountain views, golf courses, a private ski club connection, and a mix of custom homes, cabins, and estate properties. Homes here tend to be large—3,500 to 8,000+ square feet—with high-end finishes: stone countertops, wide-plank hardwood, vaulted great rooms, multiple fireplaces, and luxury bathrooms with high-end tile and glass.

Second-home owners who visit for ski weekends, golf weekends, and summer family stays want to arrive to a home that is fully clean and ready. That means the cleaning standard has to be consistent and reliable whether the home has been vacant for two weeks or two months.

Second-home logistics at Promontory

Many Promontory homes are managed remotely. Owners coordinate cleaning through a property manager, a keybox, or a gate access system. Sun Ray is experienced working with remote-managed properties—scheduling and confirmations happen via text or email, access instructions are followed precisely, and a photo check-out summary can be provided after each visit.

Vacant-home dust accumulation is a real issue at Promontory’s elevation. Horizontal surfaces, vents, and window sills collect fine mineral dust quickly. A home that is cleaned monthly requires less time per visit than one cleaned only on arrival—and it costs less over the course of a year.

Cleaning standards for luxury finishes

Stone countertops and tile require pH-neutral cleaners—not the acidic or bleach-based products that damage sealants. Hardwood floors need dry or lightly damp mops, not saturated mops that cause warping. Glass shower enclosures in hard-water areas need specific lime-scale removers applied carefully to avoid etching. Sun Ray uses products appropriate to each surface type and checks with homeowners before using anything new on specialty finishes.

Recurring vs. arrival-only cleaning at Promontory

Many Promontory owners start with arrival-only cleans (before each visit) and upgrade to monthly recurring service after experiencing the difference. A monthly schedule keeps the home in a maintained state, reduces the per-visit scope, and means the home is always ready rather than cleaned on a reactive basis when guests are already en route.
