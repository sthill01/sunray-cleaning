What a Park City Airbnb Turnover Clean Includes

A turnover clean is the guest-ready reset between checkout and the next arrival. For Park City hosts, the scope needs to cover the visible details guests review first and the operational details that keep the calendar moving.

A good Park City Airbnb turnover clean focuses on kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, floors, trash, high-touch surfaces, entryways, guest presentation and the host checklist. Laundry, supply restocking, hot tubs, inside appliances and deep-clean items should be clarified before the quote.

The direct answer

A Park City Airbnb turnover clean can include cleaning the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas and floors; removing trash; wiping high-touch surfaces; resetting visible guest spaces; making beds when requested; handling towels or linens when included; checking supplies when provided; and flagging obvious damage or guest-left issues. The exact scope depends on the home, booking window and host expectations.

Kitchen and dining areas

Guests notice the kitchen immediately. A turnover clean usually includes counters, sink, appliance exteriors, stovetop, microwave touchups, cabinet fronts where needed, floors, trash, visible dishes and the dining area. Inside ovens, refrigerator interiors, heavy grease and pantry organization should be requested as add-ons or deep-clean items.

Bathrooms and bedrooms

Bathrooms need a full reset: toilets, showers, tubs, sinks, mirrors, counters, floors, fixtures and fresh towels when supplied. Bedrooms usually include dusting, floors, high-touch surfaces and bed-making when the host provides the linen process. If laundry is part of the scope, the quote should say whether it is onsite, offsite, owner-managed or cleaner-managed.

Living spaces, entryways and high-touch details

Living rooms, mudrooms and entryways matter in Park City because guests track in snow, dust, gear and summer trail debris. Turnover cleaning should address floors, tables, remotes, door handles, switches, railings, boot areas, visible glass and the arrival impression.

What is usually outside a standard turnover?

Deep-clean tasks are different from a normal guest reset. Baseboards, heavy hard-water buildup, inside appliances, detailed window cleaning, wall marks, upholstery stains, carpet cleaning, post-construction dust and major guest damage need extra time and should be quoted separately.

Same-day turnover checklist for hosts

  • Confirm checkout and check-in times before the cleaning day.
  • Share parking, gate, lockbox or smart-lock instructions.
  • Clarify laundry expectations before the quote.
  • Send the host checklist and photos of any priority spaces.
  • Note pets, hot tubs, supply restocking, guest damage or unusual messes.
  • Build extra time around holidays, Sundance, ski weekends and back-to-back bookings.

FAQs

Does a turnover clean include laundry?
It can, but laundry expectations should be confirmed before the quote. Onsite laundry, offsite linen handling, towel staging and bed-making all affect timing.
Does a turnover clean include restocking supplies?
Supply checks or restocking can be included when supplies are provided and the expectations are clear. Hosts should share the checklist before the clean.
Is a turnover clean the same as a deep clean?
No. A turnover clean resets the home between guests. A deep clean handles heavier buildup and detailed tasks that need more time.
Can Sun Ray help with same-day turnovers in Park City?
Same-day availability depends on the date, home size, condition, route, access and laundry scope. Send checkout and check-in times when requesting a quote.
