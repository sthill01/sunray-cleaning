Park City house cleaning typically runs $150–$380 for a standard clean, with wide variation based on home size, condition, frequency, and service type. Here is what affects the quote.

Typical pricing ranges for Park City homes

A standard recurring clean for a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom Park City home generally falls between $150 and $220. Larger homes—four bedrooms and above, which are common in Deer Valley, Promontory, and Park Meadows—typically run $220–$380. First-time cleans and deep cleans cost more because they address buildup that routine visits do not.

Homes left vacant between ski and summer seasons often need extra time due to dust accumulation, hard water deposits, and oxidation on stainless and chrome surfaces. That translates directly into labor hours and higher quotes.

What makes Park City cleaning different from Salt Lake pricing

Park City sits at 6,900–7,200 feet elevation. That altitude brings drier air, harder water, and fine mineral dust that settles on surfaces faster than in lower valleys. Hard water deposits on shower glass and chrome fixtures require specific products and more time to remove properly. Many Park City homeowners—especially second-home owners who visit on weekends or during ski season—also expect a higher standard of detail on arrival.

Travel time also factors in. Cleaners who base operations in the Park City area can quote efficiently. Services based in Salt Lake City often add a travel surcharge for Summit and Wasatch County homes.

What affects your quote most

Square footage and bedroom/bathroom count drive the base quote. Current condition adds time: a home that has been vacant for several weeks, hosted a large family gathering, or has pets will need more attention than a home that receives regular care. Frequency of service is a major factor—weekly or biweekly recurring clients typically pay 10–20% less per visit than one-time customers because the home stays in a consistent, manageable state between visits.

Add-on services like inside-oven cleaning, inside refrigerator, interior windows, or laundry folding are priced separately and listed clearly in advance.

FAQs about Park City house cleaning pricing

What is the average cost for house cleaning in Park City?
Most Park City homes pay $150–$380 per standard clean depending on size, condition, and frequency. Recurring clients typically pay less per visit than one-time bookings.
Why does my first clean cost more?
First-time cleans address accumulated buildup—hard water deposits, grout, vents, and areas that haven't been detailed in months. Once the home is at a maintained baseline, recurring visits take less time and cost less.
Does Sun Ray charge extra for vacation homes that have been closed up?
Yes, vacant homes often need extra time due to dust, oxidation, and mineral deposits. Sun Ray will walk through the scope with you and quote accordingly before booking.
