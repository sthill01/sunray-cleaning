Midway as a vacation rental market

Midway occupies the eastern end of Heber Valley, about 45 minutes from Salt Lake City. Its draw includes Homestead Resort (with a unique geothermal crater pool), Wasatch Mountain State Park for hiking and golf, Deer Creek State Park and Reservoir for boating, and easy access to Soldier Hollow for cross-country skiing in winter. Summer and fall are peak seasons for many Midway hosts, complemented by a growing winter market as skiers discover the area's proximity to Deer Valley and Park City Mountain.

Midway STR properties range from Swiss-inspired chalets and cabins to newer luxury homes and condominiums in communities like Interlaken, Crowne Pointe, and Swiss Mountain. Guest expectations have risen alongside nightly rates, and a professionally cleaned home is now table stakes for competitive listings.

Turnover priorities specific to Midway

Midway guests tend to be outdoor-activity-focused. That means muddy boots from Wasatch Mountain trails, wet gear from Deer Creek kayaking or paddle sports, and dogs accompanying hiking guests. Entryways, mudrooms, and hard floors see heavier use than in a purely ski-centric property.

Heber Valley water is hard, similar to Park City. Shower glass and chrome fixtures accumulate lime and calcium deposits that need descaling attention during turnovers and deeper cleaning during seasonal resets. Midway homes on well water (more common than in Park City) may have even higher mineral content, requiring more frequent descaling work.

Scheduling around Midway’s year-round season

Unlike Park City, which has two clear peak seasons, Midway sees relatively consistent demand year-round. Summer is the strongest season, followed by ski-adjacent winter weeks and fall leaf-season weekends. This makes recurring cleaning relationships more valuable than one-off seasonal bookings—hosts benefit from a reliable cleaner available across all seasons.

How do I set up reliable turnover cleaning for a Midway property I manage remotely?
Contact Sun Ray to set up a recurring turnover arrangement. Most remote hosts share keybox access, a guest checkout schedule, and linen/supply preferences once, and Sun Ray handles the rest. Updates happen by text or email.
