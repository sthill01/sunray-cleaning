## Intro (direct answer)

Move-in/move-out cleaning is most stressful when timing is tight — keys change hands, inspections happen, and you need the home to feel “reset,” not just lightly wiped down.

## The move clean “scope trap” (avoid this)

Before you hire any cleaner, clarify:

- inside appliances (fridge/oven) included or not
- inside cabinets included or not
- baseboards and vents included or not
- any heavy buildup areas that require extra time

Move cleans vary a lot — it’s better to align upfront than to be surprised at the end.

## Move-out cleaning checklist (room-by-room)

### Kitchen

- counters and backsplash cleaned and sanitized
- sink and faucet cleaned; water spots reduced
- appliance exteriors wiped (fridge, dishwasher, oven)
- inside microwave wiped
- cabinet fronts wiped (especially around handles)
- floors vacuumed/mopped

Optional (confirm scope):

- inside fridge
- inside oven
- inside cabinets/drawers

### Bathrooms

- toilet sanitized (seat, rim, base, handle)
- sink/counter cleaned; hair removed
- mirror cleaned streak-free
- shower/tub cleaned (buildup addressed as needed)
- floors vacuumed/mopped

### Bedrooms and living areas

- dust surfaces and corners
- wipe baseboards (especially in traffic areas)
- vacuum floors and edges
- spot-check walls/doors for fingerprints

### Entry + mudroom

- wipe high-touch points (handles, switches)
- sweep/vacuum grit and corners
- clean visible smudges on doors

## Move-in cleaning checklist (the “fresh start” version)

Even if a home looks clean, move-in cleaning focuses on the places you’ll touch immediately:

- bathrooms (full reset)
- kitchen sink, counters, appliance handles
- inside fridge if needed
- floors and baseboards in main areas
- bedrooms (dust + floors)

If you’re moving into a vacation home, consider a deeper reset before your first stay.

## FAQs

### Is move-out cleaning the same as a deep clean?

Often similar, but not always. Move cleans may focus on specific scope items (inside appliances, inside cabinets, baseboards) depending on the home and the deadline.

### Should I book a move-in clean even if the home “looks clean”?

Yes — a move-in clean is a fresh-start reset that focuses on hygiene and high-touch areas, especially kitchens and bathrooms.
