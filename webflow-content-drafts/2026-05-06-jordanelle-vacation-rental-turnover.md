## What makes Jordanelle turnovers different?

Three common Jordanelle issues that affect guest reviews:

1. **Water spots + hard-water residue** on showers, faucets, sinks, and glass.
2. **Sand, trail dust, and grit** tracked in from the reservoir, boat ramps, hiking, and parking areas.

## A simple turnover timeline (so you do not run late)

If your check-out/check-in window is 4-6 hours, prioritize:

- Bathrooms first (trust zone)
- Kitchen second (attention zone)
- Beds/linens third (first-impression zone)
- Floors last (final reveal)

If you have back-to-back bookings, consider two tiers:

## Jordanelle turnover cleaning checklist (arrival-ready)

### Entry + first impressions

- Empty trash and replace liners
- Shake out and vacuum entry mats
- Wipe high-touch surfaces (handles, switches, thermostat)
- Quick scan for guest-left items

### Bedrooms

- Strip and remake beds with fresh linens (or reset existing if owner-supplied process)
- Wipe nightstands and lamp bases
- Dust obvious surfaces and picture frames
- Vacuum under bed edges if accessible
- Ensure extra linens are staged (if part of your standard)

### Bathrooms (the trust zone)

- Sanitize toilet (seat, rim, handle, base area)
- Clean sinks/counters and remove hair
- Clean mirrors streak-free
- Remove water spots on fixtures and shower glass where possible
- Replace used soap/shampoo (if you provide supplies)
- Restock toilet paper and make it visible
- Mop bathroom floors last

### Kitchen (the attention zone)

- Clean and sanitize counters and backsplash
- Clean sink and faucet; remove obvious water spots
- Wipe cabinet fronts around handles (fingerprint hotspots)
- Wipe appliance exteriors (fridge, dishwasher, microwave)
- Check inside microwave; wipe splatter
- Confirm the trash area is clean and odor-free

### Living areas

- Vacuum couches/chairs and check between cushions
- Wipe coffee table and high-touch surfaces (remote, side tables)
- Clean fingerprints on glass doors and windows at eye level
- Straighten throws/pillows so it looks intentional

### Floors (final reveal)

- Vacuum edges/corners and main traffic paths
- Mop hard floors, especially entry and kitchen

### Optional add-ons (common Jordanelle requests)

- Hot tub wipe-down / patio reset (if applicable)
- Fireplace glass exterior wipe (if used)
- Light patio furniture wipe and sweep (wind and dust happen fast near the reservoir)
