## What’s included in Park City turnover cleaning (arrival-ready)

Every property is different, but most Park City turnovers include:

### Bathrooms (trust zone)

- sanitize toilet, sink, counters, high-touch points
- clean mirrors and obvious glass streak-free
- clean shower/tub surfaces and fixtures
- empty trash and replace liners
- vacuum/mop floors

### Kitchen (attention zone)

- sanitize counters and backsplash
- clean sink and faucet; reduce water spots where possible
- wipe appliance exteriors (fridge/dishwasher/microwave fronts)
- check inside microwave; wipe splatter
- wipe cabinet fronts around handles (fingerprint hotspots)

### Bedrooms + living areas

- dust obvious surfaces and high-visibility areas
- vacuum upholstery and check between cushions
- wipe tables, remotes, high-touch surfaces
- vacuum/mop floors (final reveal)

### Final walk-through (review protection)

- quick visual scan from the entry
- confirm bathrooms + kitchen + beds look “first-arrival” ready

Optional add-ons (quote-dependent):

- linens/laundry support and bed making
- restocking consumables (owner-supplied list)
- inside fridge/oven (when needed)
- heavy glass/detail work
- hot tub wipe-down/patio reset (property dependent)
