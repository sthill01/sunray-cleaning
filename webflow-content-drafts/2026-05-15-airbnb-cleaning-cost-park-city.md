## The 7 biggest things that change Park City turnover pricing

### 1) Beds and baths (workload multiplier)

Bathrooms take time. A 3-bed property with 3 bathrooms is usually a bigger turnover than a 3-bed with 2 bathrooms.

### 2) Linens and laundry (the hidden time cost)

If the cleaner is responsible for:

- stripping/remaking beds
- washing/drying
- staging towels

…the scope increases. The same is true if laundry is slow, machines are limited, or the home needs multiple loads per turnover.

### 3) Same-day window pressure

Turnovers are often “10am out / 4pm in.” When timing is tight, teams may need to staff differently to guarantee an on-time reset.

### 4) Condition and guest behavior

Most turnovers are routine. But heavier mess, sticky kitchens, or bathroom buildup turns a turnover into a deeper clean.

### 5) Property features that add steps

Common Park City add-ons:

- hot tubs / patio resets (property dependent)
- heavy glass and large sliding doors
- multiple floors/stairs
- fireplaces and ash/soot concerns (varies by property)

### 6) Restocking and consumables

If your cleaner is expected to restock coffee, toiletries, paper products, trash bags, etc., make sure it’s in the scope with a clear list and storage instructions.

### 7) Proof and accountability expectations

Some hosts want:

- a checklist confirmation
- photos after cleaning
- damage/issue flagging

That’s often worth it — but it changes workflow and cost.

## How to set your Airbnb/VRBO cleaning fee without hurting reviews

Three practical rules:

1. Don’t underprice the clean and then expect luxury results.
2. Treat the bathroom + kitchen as the “review zones” — build time for them.
3. Schedule a deeper reset periodically so small misses don’t accumulate.

If you run a high-end Park City rental, “cheap” turnovers often cost more later (bad reviews, emergency clean calls, last-minute swaps).

## FAQs

### Is Park City Airbnb cleaning more expensive than a normal house clean?

Often, yes. Turnovers are time-sensitive and can include presentation work (beds/linens), restocking, and checklist reliability.

### Can I get a flat rate per turnover?

Many hosts prefer a flat rate for predictability, but it still needs to reflect real property size and scope.

### How often should I schedule a deep clean for a Park City STR?

Common cadence is seasonal (pre-ski, post-ski, pre-summer) plus as-needed when buildup starts showing up.
