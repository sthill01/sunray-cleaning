## What a “deep clean” usually includes (vs standard cleaning)

Standard/maintenance cleaning focuses on keeping a home tidy and hygienic week-to-week.

Deep cleaning focuses on:

- buildup
- detail zones
- “why does it still feel dusty?” areas
- the things guests and homeowners notice immediately

In Park City / Heber / Midway homes, common deep-clean priorities are:

- hard-water spots and bathroom buildup
- baseboards and corners (dust + grit)
- kitchen grease, sticky cabinet fronts, and fingerprints
- glass doors and large window areas
- vents and high surfaces (seasonal dust)

## The 6 biggest things that change deep-clean pricing

### 1) Home size and bathroom count

Bathrooms drive labor. More baths usually means more deep-clean time.

### 2) Current condition (buildup vs maintained)

A “seasonal reset” is different from a first deep clean after months of heavy use or long vacancy.

### 3) Kitchen detail level

Some deep cleans include only visible surfaces; others include heavy detail on:

- cabinet fronts
- backsplash and grout lines
- inside microwave
- heavy grease zones

### 4) Glass and water spots

Park City and Heber homes often have large windows and glass doors. Water spots and streak-free glass add time.

### 5) Baseboards, vents, and high dusting

If you want vents, fans, blinds, and higher surfaces addressed, specify it — it’s worth it, but it changes scope.

### 6) Inside appliances (optional but common)

Inside fridge/oven cleaning is often requested for:

- move-ins
- seasonal openings
- “the home is clean but doesn’t feel clean” situations

## Deep cleaning vs recurring cleaning: which should you book first?

Practical rule:

- If the home hasn’t been detailed recently, start with a deep clean.
- If the home is maintained but you want it to stay guest-ready, start recurring cleaning after the deep clean.

This approach prevents “deep clean problems” from becoming permanent.

## FAQs

### What’s included in a deep clean?

Deep cleans focus on detail zones (bathrooms, kitchens, baseboards, buildup, and areas that standard cleaning doesn’t fully reset). Exact scope varies by home, so it’s best to confirm priorities during quoting.

### How long does a deep clean take?

Yes — especially at season transitions (post-ski, pre-summer, pre-ski). A deep reset before guest traffic helps protect reviews and reduce last-minute stress.
