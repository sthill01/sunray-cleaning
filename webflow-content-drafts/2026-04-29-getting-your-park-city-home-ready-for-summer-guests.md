1. **Mountain dust and pollen buildup** on floors, baseboards, vents, and window sills.

## A simple timeline: when to clean before guests arrive

### 7-10 days before arrival (the deep reset window)

Best for: second homes, homes that have been closed up, or any place that hasn't had a real detail clean recently.

- Deep dust: baseboards, window sills, ceiling fans, vents
- Full bathroom reset: showers/tubs, grout lines, fixtures, mirrors
- Kitchen detail: fronts of cabinets, backsplash, sink, appliance exteriors
- Floors: vacuum edges and corners; mop hard floors thoroughly
- Bedroom refresh: under-bed dust, nightstands, lamp bases, mirrors

### 24-48 hours before arrival (the "polish pass")

Best for: homes that are already generally maintained.

- Re-wipe high-touch surfaces (handles, switches, remotes, counters)
- Freshen bathrooms (toilets, sinks, mirrors, floors)
- Re-check kitchen sink, counters, and stove surface
- Final floor pass (especially entry + kitchen + hallways)
- Quick patio tidy and glass spot-check

### Turnover day (short-term rentals)

Best for: tight changeovers where timing matters.

- Sanitize bathrooms and kitchen surfaces
- Reset beds/linens and make the home look "first-arrival" ready
- Empty trash and replace liners
- Restock essentials (if you use a restock list)
- Final walk-through: entry, living room, bathrooms, kitchen, patio

## Park City summer guest-ready checklist (room-by-room)

### Entry + mudroom

- Shake out mats and vacuum edges
- Wipe door handles, light switches, and railings
- Clear guest storage space (hooks, bench, shelf)
- Quick sweep for trail dust and grit

### Kitchen (guest attention zone)

- Clean sink and faucet; remove water spots
- Wipe counters and backsplash
- Wipe appliance exteriors (especially fridge and dishwasher fronts)
- Sanitize handles (fridge, oven, cabinets)
- Check inside microwave; wipe crumbs and splatter

### Bathrooms (trust zone)

- Remove hard-water spots from fixtures and glass where possible
- Sanitize toilet, seat, handle, and surrounding floor edges
- Clean mirror streak-free
- Wipe counters and drawer fronts
- Replace hand soap and ensure extra toilet paper is visible

### Bedrooms + living areas

- Dust high-visibility surfaces (nightstands, coffee tables, lamp bases)
- Vacuum edges, under seating, and corners
- Clean fingerprints on glass and mirrors
- Make sure throws/pillows look intentional (not "lived in")

### Patio + outdoor areas

- Wipe patio table and chairs
- Quick sweep of entry steps / porch
- Spot-clean glass doors and railings
- If grilling: wipe exterior surfaces and remove obvious mess

## FAQs

### How far in advance should I schedule a pre-guest cleaning in Park City?

If you want a deeper reset, schedule 7-10 days ahead. For a quick polish clean, 24-48 hours ahead is usually enough - but summer weekends book up fast.

### What's the biggest difference between winter and summer cleaning in Park City?

Summer tends to mean more outdoor dust, patio mess, and fingerprints from higher traffic, while winter is more salt/grit and wet gear. The guest-visible zones (kitchen and bathrooms) still matter most year-round.
