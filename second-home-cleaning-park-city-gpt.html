Cleaning around your stays

A practical cleaning plan for a home that is not occupied every day.

Second homes collect dust, hard-water spots, entry grit, and stale-room buildup even during quiet weeks. The right plan depends on how the property is used and how you want it to feel when you open the door.

Before owner arrival

Focus the clean on kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, floors, entry areas, dust, and the rooms your household will use first.

After departure

Reset the home after an owner or family stay so the next visit starts from a known, clean condition.

Recurring upkeep

Use a weekly, biweekly, or monthly cleaning plan when regular maintenance fits the home, travel schedule, and route availability.

Seasonal deep clean

Plan deeper attention around ski-season grit, summer dust, guest visits, long vacancies, or a return after months away.

Choose the right scope

Second-home cleaning is not one fixed checklist.

A lightly used condo before a weekend arrival may need a different plan than a larger home after holiday guests. Sun Ray builds the quote around the actual rooms, condition, access, timing, and priorities.

  • Routine home cleaningKitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living spaces, floors, dusting, and high-touch areas.
  • Deeper resetUse deep cleaning when buildup, seasonal dust, baseboards, fixtures, or long gaps between visits need more attention.
  • Guest-ready visitTell Sun Ray when family or guests are arriving and which spaces need to be ready first.
  • New-home transitionUse move-in cleaning when a recently purchased Park City home needs a reset before belongings arrive.

Direct answers

Second-home cleaning questions from Park City owners.

Does Sun Ray Cleaning clean second homes in Park City?
Yes. Sun Ray offers quote-based cleaning for Park City second homes, including owner-arrival cleaning, post-departure resets, recurring upkeep, seasonal deep cleaning, and guest-ready visits when schedule and property scope fit.
Can I schedule cleaning before I arrive at my Park City home?
Yes, subject to availability. Send the property location, home size, requested clean date, arrival time, current condition, access plan, and priority rooms so Sun Ray can confirm the scope and timing.
Can a second home be cleaned while it is vacant?
A vacant second home can be cleaned when access and scope are confirmed. Explain how long the home has been empty, whether anyone used it recently, and what should be ready before the next arrival.
Is second-home cleaning the same as Airbnb turnover cleaning?
Not always. A private second home may need owner-arrival, post-departure, recurring, or seasonal cleaning. A short-term rental turnover is planned around guest checkout, check-in, and a host-approved turnover scope. Sun Ray will match the quote to the way the property is used.
Does this service include property management or home-watch inspections?
This page describes residential cleaning, not full property management or a home-watch program. Ask about any non-cleaning request before booking so Sun Ray can confirm what is and is not included.
How much does second-home cleaning cost in Park City?
Sun Ray uses property-specific quotes. Price depends on home size, rooms, condition, cleaning depth, timing, access, pets, frequency, and requested priorities. Send those details for a practical estimate.
